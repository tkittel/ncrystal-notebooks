Jupyter notebooks with examples, documentation and tutorials for
[NCrystal](https://github.com/mctools/ncrystal). Each notebook can be read
here, downloaded (in versions for installations with pip or conda), or opened
directly in Google Colab, using the links at the top of its page.

The notebooks are maintained in the
[ncrystal-notebooks](https://github.com/mctools/ncrystal-notebooks)
repository. Contributions of new notebooks are very welcome, see the
[developer section](developers.md).
