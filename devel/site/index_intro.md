Welcome to the notebooks of [NCrystal](https://github.com/mctools/ncrystal),
the library for thermal neutron transport in crystals, liquids and amorphous
materials. NCrystal works as a backend in Monte Carlo codes like OpenMC, McStas
and Geant4, on the command line, and through its C, C++ and Python APIs. Here,
Jupyter notebooks show what you can do with it from Python, from first steps to
advanced material modelling. Read them here, download them (for pip or conda
installations), or open them directly in Google Colab, using the links at the
top of each page.

The notebooks are maintained in the
[ncrystal-notebooks](https://github.com/mctools/ncrystal-notebooks)
repository. Contributions of new notebooks are very welcome, see the
[developer section](developers.md).
